1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners. Cookies are not programs — they cannot install software or carry viruses.

We also use similar technologies such as session tokens (stored server-side) and local storage for equivalent purposes.

2. Cookies We Use

We use three categories of cookies:

Essential Strictly Necessary

These cookies are required for the Service to function. They cannot be disabled. They include:

  • Your login session token (keeps you signed in between page loads)
  • CSRF security tokens (protects against cross-site attacks)
Functional Preference Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences to improve your experience:

  • Theme preference (light / dark mode)
  • Dashboard UI state preferences
Analytics Performance & Analytics

These help us understand how the Service is being used so we can improve it. All data collected is anonymised and aggregated:

  • Page visit frequency and navigation paths
  • Error rates and performance metrics

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use Razorpay for processing payments. When you visit the checkout page, Razorpay may set its own cookies on your device for fraud prevention and payment security. These cookies are subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy.

Other than Razorpay (on checkout pages only), we do not allow any third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies on our platform.

4. How to Control Cookies

You can control and manage cookies in several ways:

  • Browser Settings: Most browsers allow you to view, delete, and block cookies. Note that blocking essential cookies will prevent you from logging in or using the dashboard.
  • Private Mode: Most session cookies are automatically deleted when you close a private browsing session.
