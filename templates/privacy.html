1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Bord AI ("we", "us", "our"). We operate blockbord.ai and the Block Bord AI browser extension (collectively, the "Service"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how it is used, and what rights you have in relation to it. Please read it carefully — by using the Service you agree to the terms described below.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the Service. This includes:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your username and email address. Passwords are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes (bcrypt) — we cannot recover them.
  • Scan Content: URLs, text messages, or page content you submit for analysis via the dashboard or the browser extension.
  • Browser Telemetry (Extension only): When the extension is active, it collects anonymised signals such as JavaScript obfuscation indicators. This data is not linked to your browsing history.
  • Usage & Log Data: Standard server logs including IP addresses, browser type, and timestamps.
  • Payment Information: Payments are processed via Razorpay. We store only the Razorpay order ID and payment status.
  • Feedback Data: If you label a scan as "spam" or "safe", we store that label to improve our AI model.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide real-time phishing and threat detection services.
  • To maintain your personal scan history and dashboard.
  • To enforce daily scan limits based on your subscription plan.
  • To retrain and improve our AI threat detection models using anonymised, aggregated feedback.
  • To send transactional emails (e.g., high-risk detection alerts) if opted in.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We share your data with a limited number of trusted third-party services necessary to operate the platform:

  • Razorpay — Payment processing. Subject to Razorpay's Privacy Policy.
  • SMTP Email Provider — Used to send security alert emails.
  • Hosting Infrastructure — Servers hosting the application.

We may disclose your data if required by law, court order, or government authority.

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. Scan history is retained for a rolling period of 12 months after which older entries may be purged. You can delete your account at any time by contacting us.

6. Security Measures

We take security seriously and implement multiple layers of protection:

  • All passwords are hashed using bcrypt.
  • All data in transit is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • CSRF tokens protect all state-changing HTTP requests.
  • Rate limiting protects all authentication and scan endpoints against brute-force and abuse.

No system is 100% secure. In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you in accordance with applicable law.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Portability: Request your scan history in a machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, please visit our Contact page.

8. Children's Privacy

Block Bord AI is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes by email and will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please contact us via our Contact page. We aim to respond to all privacy-related enquiries within 5 business days.
