1. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to protect you. This includes:

  • Account Info: Email address and hashed passwords (we never store plain-text).
  • Scan Payloads: URLs, text, or content you submit for analysis.
  • Telemetry: Anonymized threat signals from the browser extension.
  • Payment: Verification data from Razorpay (we do not store credit card numbers).

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide and improve the Service:

  • Generating real-time forensic risk reports.
  • Maintaining your scan history dashboard.
  • Aggregating anonymized feedback to train our threat models.
  • Preventing platform abuse and fraud.

3. Data Sharing

We do NOT sell or rent your personal data. We share information only with trusted processors necessary for operations (e.g., Razorpay for payments, transactional email providers).

4. Data Retention

We retain account data as long as your profile is active. Scan history is retained for a rolling period of 12 months. Users may request full account deletion via the dashboard or support.

5. Security Measures

We implement industry-standard encryption (TLS) for all data in transit. Accounts are protected by industry-standard hashing and optional multi-factor authentication.
