Cookie Policy

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are used to make websites work efficiently and to provide session management. We also use local storage and session tokens for equivalent purposes.

2. Cookies we use

Essential Strictly Necessary

These are required for the platform to function. They include session tokens (to keep you logged in) and security tokens (to prevent CSRF attacks).

Functional Preferences

These remember your interface choices, such as your UI layout preferences and dashboard state.

Analytics Performance

These help us understand how users interact with the Service. All data is anonymized and used solely for performance monitoring.

3. Control

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling essential cookies will prevent you from signing in or using the dashboard.
