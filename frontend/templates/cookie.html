Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by your web browser when you visit websites. They allow platforms to remember your authentication session, preserve preference settings, and safeguard against security threats.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, Block Bord AI uses modern HTML5 Web Storage (localStorage and sessionStorage) to securely handle transient application state without storing tracking scripts.

Categories of Cookies We Use

Below is a complete index of cookie types utilized across our website and applications:

Category Purpose & Functionality Lifespan
Essential / Session Used to keep you securely logged in, verify CSRF security tokens, and maintain active user sessions (`session_id`). Session (purged on logout)
Security & Abuse Detects rapid automated bot activity, prevents rate-limit bypassing, and validates API keys across endpoints. 1 day to 30 days
Third-Party Payment Set securely by Razorpay checkout frames to process payments, validate checkout sessions, and prevent fraud. Varies by Razorpay policy

Managing & Disabling Cookies

You have full control to accept, block, or clear cookies directly within your web browser preferences. Please note that disabling essential session cookies will prevent you from signing into your dashboard or utilizing paid API features.

Google Chrome
Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and site data
Mozilla Firefox
Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies & Site Data
Apple Safari
Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
Microsoft Edge
Settings → Cookies and Site Permissions
