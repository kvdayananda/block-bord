What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

Types of Cookies We Use

  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how you use the platform so we can improve it. We use anonymized data only.
  • Security Cookies: Protect against fraud and ensure safe transactions.

Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies may limit your ability to log in or use certain features.

We embed third‑party services such as Razorpay for payments. These providers may set their own cookies according to their policies.
