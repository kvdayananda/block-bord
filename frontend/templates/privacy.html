Privacy Policy

Block Bord AI ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and disclose personal information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address, username, and password (hashed).
  • Usage data: IP address, browser type, pages visited, and interaction timestamps.
  • Security data: URLs, files, or text you submit for analysis, stored temporarily for processing.

How We Use Your Data

  • Provide and improve the AI security services.
  • Send transactional emails (e.g., password resets, payment receipts).
  • Detect and mitigate abuse or fraudulent activity.

Data Retention

Analysis data is retained for a maximum of 48 hours and then permanently deleted. Account data is retained until you delete your account.

Sharing of Information

  • We never sell your data to third parties.
  • We may share anonymized usage statistics for research.
  • We may disclose data to comply with legal obligations or protect rights.

Your Rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data by contacting support.
  • Opt‑out of marketing communications via the unsubscribe link in emails.

Security

All data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Passwords are stored using strong salted hashing.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact us.
