1. Overview & Our Commitment

Block Bord AI ("we", "us", "our") is dedicated to safeguarding your privacy and treating all user data with maximum security. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, store, and protect personal and security data when you access our platform, APIs, or browser tools.

Zero Data Monetization Guarantee: We never sell, monetize, or rent your personal information, scan logs, or target submission inputs to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. Information We Collect

We limit collection strictly to data essential for platform operations and security threat analysis:

  • Account Credentials: Email address, username, and password hashes generated using Argon2id encryption.
  • Security Target Inputs: URLs, IP addresses, file hashes, QR codes, or text patterns submitted for threat assessment.
  • Telemetry & Usage Data: Anonymized IP addresses, HTTP user-agent headers, timestamp logs, and API token usage statistics to prevent platform abuse and enforce tier rate limits.
  • Billing Metadata: Transaction reference IDs and subscription status provided securely by payment processors (Razorpay). We do not store raw credit card details on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Data

Your information is processed strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Executing real-time 3-layer risk inspection (Safe Browsing API + XGBoost ML Engine + Heuristic Rules).
  • Generating threat assessment reports, history logs, and security dashboard metrics.
  • Authenticating user sessions (JWT & 2FA) and verifying API access permissions.
  • Mitigating rate-limit abuse, DDoS attacks, and malicious automated bot activity.
  • Sending essential account notifications such as password resets and payment receipts.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Transient security scan inputs (e.g. submitted URLs or files) are stored in encrypted temporary caches during processing and automatically purged within 48 hours unless saved explicitly to a registered user’s scan history.

Account data remains active for the duration of your registration. When you choose to close your account, all associated session records, API keys, and personal identifiers are permanently deleted from our primary databases within 30 days.

5. Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not share your private data except under the following strict scenarios:

  • Infrastructure Partners: Secure cloud infrastructure providers (e.g. Neon PostgreSQL, Render hosting) bound by strict non-disclosure agreements.
  • Anonymized Threat Metrics: Aggregated, fully non-identifiable threat statistics (e.g., total phishing domains blocked) used for public security reports.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by enforceable law enforcement sub-poenas or statutory legal obligations.

6. Your Data Rights & Choices

Under global privacy frameworks (GDPR, CCPA, and DPDP Act), you possess full authority over your data:

  • Right to Access: Export a full archive of your account history and scan logs at any time.
  • Right to Erasure: Delete your account and associated telemetry upon request.
  • Right to Rectification: Update your profile credentials and security settings directly from the account dashboard.

7. Technical Security Measures

All network communications are encrypted end-to-end using TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords and secrets are protected via Argon2id hashing algorithms, and session tokens utilize cryptographically signed JWTs with strict expiration lifetimes.

8. Data Protection Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your privacy rights, our Data Protection Team is available to assist you.
